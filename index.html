About Us

Quality Textiles at Your Doorstep

Arihant Textiles is Rourkela's trusted destination for premium quality fabrics and textiles. Located at Power House Road, Udit Nagar, we offer a wide range of sarees, dress materials, suiting, shirting, and more — all at competitive prices.

From everyday wear to festive occasions, we stock fabrics for every need. Our knowledgeable staff will help you find exactly what you're looking for, whether you're stitching new outfits or looking for the perfect gift.
